# NeuroPed OBS-10 — Partner discussion kit

Editorial presentation 1.0.0 · Product reference 1.4.0. No registration, tracking cookies, analytics or automatic sending on this page.

## Status and purpose

Observational product · external pilot proposed

No product-specific clinical validation. No diagnosis or automated video interpretation. This page is not an assessment and does not accept patient data.

Preparation and review are outside the ten-minute interaction. Translating this presentation does not translate or validate the clinical protocol, which remains in Portuguese.

## What the product offers

### Prepare

An age-specific kit, safe material alternatives, authorization and audio/framing checks.

### Observe

One task at a time, clear instructions, documented assistance and the option to refuse or stop.

### Link

In the clinical product: select the authorized local video and mark the segment linked to the record.

### Review

Compare the instruction, reported history, observed response and professional comment without erasing the original note.

## What has not been demonstrated

Clinical effectiveness, diagnostic accuracy, time savings, international recognition or product-specific regulatory certification. No external partnership is announced here.

## Pilot entry gates

1. **Simulate.** Train without patients: materials, prompts, fictional files, missing audio, simulated refusal and a mismatched video.
2. **Define governance.** Clinical lead, intended purpose, eligibility, training, consent/assent and applicable approvals before collection.
3. **Compare with usual practice.** Set success criteria before looking at results. Measure preparation, collection, review and handoff, including failures and omissions.
4. **Replicate externally.** Repeat with another assistant or service and report limitations. Only communicate benefits actually demonstrated.

## Measurements and analysis

- Total time by stage, including unmeasured periods.
- Tasks with reviewable evidence and reasons for unusable material.
- Deviations in instructions, assistance, framing and accommodation needs.
- Physician-perceived usefulness, repeated tasks and incidents.

Describe each stage separately and total observed time; report how many measurements are missing and why. Do not turn missingness into zero. Report numerators and denominators and stratify when justified. Do not confuse perceived usefulness with accuracy. An arbitrary sample size or threshold does not constitute validation.

## Criteria to complete before collection

- [ ] Clinical lead and institution; purpose: care, quality improvement or research.
- [ ] Participants, exclusions and a sample justified by the objective; do not select only people who complete tasks.
- [ ] Primary measure, denominator, comparator, success threshold and missing-data rule, defined in advance.
- [ ] Safety interruptions, suspension criteria, incident handling and decision owner.
- [ ] Devices, language, accessibility, training and agreement between reviewers.
- [ ] Authorization, access, retention, export, deletion and applicable approvals.

## Safeguards

**Who decides?** Clinical decisions belong to the physician. Refusal, missing entries or insufficient footage are not diagnoses.

**What happens to data?** This presentation does not request files or activate the camera or microphone. In the clinical product, videos and JSON must be saved separately through the institutional workflow; there is no automatic upload or health-record storage.

**Is it validated or certified?** No product-specific clinical validation is presented. Software tests are not a clinical study. Ethical and regulatory review requirements depend on intended purpose and use context.

**Do three languages mean international clinical use?** No. The presentation and discussion kit are translated. Cultural adaptation, task comprehension and local permissions require evaluation before clinical use in another language or service.
