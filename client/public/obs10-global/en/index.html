01 / OBS-10

From preparation to review, with the source intact.

The assistant records. The physician interprets. The application keeps these responsibilities separate.

01

Prepare

An age-specific kit, safe material alternatives, authorization and audio/framing checks.

02

Observe

One task at a time, clear instructions, documented assistance and the option to refuse or stop.

03

Link

In the clinical product: select the authorized local video and mark the segment linked to the record.

04

Review

Compare the instruction, reported history, observed response and professional comment without erasing the original note.

03 / OBS-10

Transparency before expansion.

A success story needs reproducible results, not invented user counts, testimonials or approval badges.

01

Implemented in the product

Guided kits and task cards; session-linked local video; referenced segments; professional review; exports and voluntary operational measurements.

02

To evaluate in real use

Feasibility with representative assistants, collection quality, total staff time, use safety and usefulness to the consultation.

03

Not demonstrated

Clinical effectiveness, diagnostic accuracy, time savings, international recognition or product-specific regulatory certification. No external partnership is announced here.

Who decides?

Clinical decisions belong to the physician. Refusal, missing entries or insufficient footage are not diagnoses.

What happens to data?

This presentation does not request files or activate the camera or microphone. In the clinical product, videos and JSON must be saved separately through the institutional workflow; there is no automatic upload or health-record storage.

Is it validated or certified?

No product-specific clinical validation is presented. Software tests are not a clinical study. Ethical and regulatory review requirements depend on intended purpose and use context.

Do three languages mean international clinical use?

No. The presentation and discussion kit are translated. Cultural adaptation, task comprehension and local permissions require evaluation before clinical use in another language or service.

04 / OBS-10

Start with a question you can measure.

Can an external team reproduce the collection process and produce useful material for a consultation? The kit structures a partner discussion; it does not authorize research or care.

01

Simulate

Train without patients: materials, prompts, fictional files, missing audio, simulated refusal and a mismatched video.

02

Define governance

Clinical lead, intended purpose, eligibility, training, consent/assent and applicable approvals before collection.

03

Compare with usual practice

Set success criteria before looking at results. Measure preparation, collection, review and handoff, including failures and omissions.

04

Replicate externally

Repeat with another assistant or service and report limitations. Only communicate benefits actually demonstrated.

What a pilot should measure

  • Total time by stage, including unmeasured periods.
  • Tasks with reviewable evidence and reasons for unusable material.
  • Deviations in instructions, assistance, framing and accommodation needs.
  • Physician-perceived usefulness, repeated tasks and incidents.
