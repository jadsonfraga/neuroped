## Full Description (Apple App Store + Google Play)

NeuroPed Escalas is the most comprehensive clinical tool for healthcare professionals working in child neurology, developmental pediatrics, and neuropsychological assessment.

Developed by Dr. Jadson Fraga Araújo Júnior, a board-certified neuropediatrician, the app brings together over 60 validated scales, tests, and inventories in one place — with detailed clinical interpretation, automatic email reports, and PDF generation for printing.

WHO IT'S FOR:
• Medical students
• Physicians (Pediatricians, Neuropediatricians, Child Psychiatrists)
• Psychologists and Neuropsychologists
• Speech therapists, Occupational therapists
• Educational psychologists and Educators

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
• Behavioral Scales — SNAP-IV, Vanderbilt, CBCL/6-18, SRS-2, M-CHAT-R/F, CARS-2, ATA, SDQ, Conners, ABC and more
• Cognitive Tests — Adapted TDE-2, Recognition Tests (colors, letters, animals, body parts, ages 2-7)
• Academic Tests — Reading, Writing & Arithmetic (ages 5-14)
• School Inventories — Executive Function, School Adaptation, Social Skills
• Self-Report Inventories — Anxiety, Depression, Self-Esteem, Quality of Life (ages 8-17)
• Medication Satisfaction Scales — Adverse effects monitoring, mg/kg calculation
• Complete Clinical Record — History, Tests, Medications, Follow-up, Timeline
• Giftedness/ASD Questionnaire — High Abilities and Giftedness (50 items, 6 blocks)

KEY FEATURES:
• Smart Filter — Search using everyday terms (restless, distracted, irritable) and find the ideal scale with golden recommendation highlight
• Clinical Decision Flowchart — Visual guide for the diagnostic pathway
• Detailed Clinical Interpretation — Full-text analysis covering all answered items
• Automatic email report upon completing each assessment
• Professional PDF generation for printing
• Works offline after installation
• Premium dark interface designed for clinical use

EDUCATIONAL SECTIONS:
• Understanding Neuropsychological Assessment
• Central Auditory Processing
• School Inventories Guide

Secure, password-protected, with professional editorial design. Ideal for offices, hospitals, and academic settings.
